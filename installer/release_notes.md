## 📁 WiFile 1.0.0: same files on all your PCs

**One click:** download **`WiFile-Setup.bat`** below and double-click it on each PC. It installs everything (Python, Tailscale) and puts WiFile in your tray.

- Starts with one shared folder: `Documents\Shared`
- **Drag any folder onto the WiFile window** to share it too. It shows up on your other PCs in `Documents\WiFile`
- Private: files go straight PC to PC over your own Tailscale network
- Nothing ever gets wiped. Deletes get moved to a `todelete` folder
- Browse and upload from your phone

👉 **Step-by-step guide with pictures:** see the [README](https://github.com/MatsuYoda-88/WiFile#readme)

> Windows may say *"Windows protected your PC"*. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
